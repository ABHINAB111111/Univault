Frequently Asked Questions
How do I rent an item from another student?
›
Browse the Rent section, tap an item, choose your duration and tap "Request Rent". The owner will receive a notification and either approve or decline. Once approved, arrange pickup directly via in-app chat.
How does Lost & Found work?
›
Use the Lost & Found tab to report a lost item or post something you found. Campus alerts are sent to all verified students. Claimants must describe the item to verify ownership before pickup is arranged.
Is payment processed inside the app?
›
No. UniVault is a peer-to-peer platform. All payments happen directly between students (cash, UPI, etc.) after agreeing on terms via chat. UniVault does not handle, hold or process any money.
Who can see my listings?
›
Only verified students at your university can see your listings. UniVault is campus-restricted — no one outside your institution can access the marketplace or your profile.
How do I improve my Trust Score?
›
Complete transactions on time, return items in good condition, respond promptly to messages, and collect positive reviews. Each successful transaction adds to your score. Disputes or overdue returns reduce it.
What if an item I rented gets damaged?
›
Raise a dispute via the Report feature in the item's detail screen. Our campus admin team will review chat history and both parties' accounts. We recommend always documenting item condition at pickup with a photo.
